Features

Everything financial and accounting a church does

Built on proper fund accounting, so the building fund stays the building fund and the books are ready for your auditors.

Fund accounting

General, building, missions and any other fund, each with its own balance. Every entry is double-entry and balanced.

Offerings & M-Pesa

Count Sunday's cash, M-Pesa, cheques and bank transfers by envelope, match the count sheet and deposit.

Donors & pledges

Giving history, pledge campaigns and year-end giving statements, kept confidential to finance staff.

Bills & approvals

Vendors, bills, reimbursements and an approval limit you set. Attach receipts and invoices as you go.

Payroll

Kenyan PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and Housing Levy built in, plus allowances, SACCO and welfare deductions and printable payslips.

Banking

Bank, M-Pesa and petty cash accounts, transfers between accounts and funds, and statement reconciliation.

Budgets & reports

Budget vs. actual, statement of financial position, statement of activities, fund activity and more.

Controls & audit trail

Roles for every office, closed periods that can't be changed, and a log of who changed what.

Members register

Members, attenders and visitors with contact details, baptism, marital status and where they live. Import from a spreadsheet.

Groups & fellowships

Induct members into fellowships, cells, choirs and ministries, give them roles and approve join requests.

Gender-sensitive

Women's and men's fellowships only take those they are for, "prefer not to say" is respected, and reports show the balance.

Sermons

Upload audio, video and notes or link a YouTube service, organised into series, straight to your members' phones.

    Privacy & control

    Built for the trust a church is given

    Open books, private giving

    Members can see the church's income, spending, budget and funds, and their own giving. Nobody else's gifts are ever shown, staff pay appears only as one total, and in Ecclesia Admin each role sees only what its office needs.

    Closed books stay closed

    Close a month or year and nothing in it can be changed. Posted entries are never deleted: a mistake is voided or corrected, and the original stays on record.

    A full audit trail

    Every change records who made it and when, ready for your auditors and your finance committee.

    Your data is yours

    Download a complete backup of your books whenever you want, from Settings.

    Questions

    Frequently asked

    Can members see the church's finances or what others give?

    Members can see a summary of the church's finances if you switch it on: income by category, spending by area, progress against the budget, fund balances and campaign progress. It shows totals only, never who gave what, and all staff costs appear as one figure. Each member also sees their own giving and statements. In Ecclesia Admin, individual giving records stay visible only to the roles you allow, such as the treasurer and bookkeeper.

    Does it work for churches outside Kenya?

    Yes. Kenya is the default (Kenya Shillings, M-Pesa, PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and Housing Levy), but you choose your country when you start and can set any currency, fiscal year and payroll rules.

    How does it handle M-Pesa?

    M-Pesa is a giving method like cash or cheque: counters record each M-Pesa gift with its transaction code. Add your paybill or till as an account, like a bank account, and reconcile its statement each month.

    We use spreadsheets today. Can we move our records over?

    Yes. Members and donors import from a spreadsheet, and you enter opening balances once for each account and fund. On the Harvest and Kingdom plans we do the import for you.

    Is the price per member?

    No. It's one price for the whole church. Member logins for Ecclesia Connect are unlimited on every plan; plans differ by the size of the register, office logins, storage and support.

    Which devices does it run on?

    Ecclesia Connect runs on Android and iPhone; Ecclesia Admin runs on Windows, Mac and Linux. Both also run in any modern browser and can be installed from it.

    Can we try it before we subscribe?

    Yes. Take the interactive tour above, or ask us for a walkthrough of a demo church.

    Is the Kenyan payroll up to date?

    The current PAYE bands, personal relief, NSSF, SHIF and Housing Levy rates come built in, and every rate is a setting your treasurer can change when the law changes.
